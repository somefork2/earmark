支持

手表上的录音没有出现在 iPhone 上？

请确认 iPhone 上已安装并打开过声签，蓝牙和 Wi‑Fi 已开启。录音会一直保存在手表上，直到 iPhone 确认收到，所以不会丢失；回到连接范围后会自动发送。也可以在手表的“iPhone”页面点“立即发送”。

转文字提示“本机不支持该语言”？

转文字使用 iPhone 本机的 Apple 语音识别，支持的语言取决于设备和系统版本。可在“设置 › 识别语言”中选择普通话、粤语或英语。首次使用时系统可能需要下载语言模型。

恢复购买

在“设置 › 完整版”中点“恢复购买”。订阅可在 iPhone 的“设置 › Apple 账户 › 订阅”中管理或取消。

联系我们

请在 github.com/somefork2/earmark/issues 提交问题，我们会尽快回复。

Support

A watch recording hasn't reached my iPhone

Make sure Earmark is installed and has been opened on the iPhone, with Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi on. Recordings stay on the watch until the iPhone confirms it saved them, so nothing is lost; they are sent automatically when the devices are back in range. You can also tap "Send now" on the watch's iPhone page.

"On-device recognition is not available" for my language

Text is made by Apple's speech recognition on your iPhone; available languages depend on the device and iOS version. Choose Mandarin, Cantonese or English in Settings › Language. The system may download a language model the first time.

Restore purchases

Settings › Full version › Restore purchase. Manage or cancel a subscription in iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

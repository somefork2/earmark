隐私政策

声签（Earmark）是 Apple Watch 和 iPhone 上的录音与语音转文字工具。我们的原则很简单：你的录音和文字只属于你。

我们不收集的内容

  • 不需要注册账号，我们没有存放你数据的服务器。
  • 录音、转写文字、标题、标签、标记和搜索内容只保存在你的 Apple Watch 和 iPhone 上，从不上传给我们。
  • 语音转文字由 iPhone 本机的 Apple 语音识别完成，音频不会发送到任何服务器。
  • 不使用广告标识符（IDFA），不做跨应用追踪，不出售任何数据。

手表与 iPhone 之间的传输

录音通过 Apple 的 WatchConnectivity 在你自己的手表和 iPhone 之间直接传输。iPhone 确认保存后，手表上的副本才会删除。

iCloud 备份（可选，默认关闭）

如果你在设置中开启 iCloud 备份，录音和文字会保存到你自己的 iCloud 账户中，由 Apple 依照其隐私政策处理。我们无法访问。

匿名使用统计（仅在你同意时）

首次打开时我们会询问你是否愿意发送匿名使用统计，你可以随时在设置中关闭。同意后，应用通过 Google Analytics for Firebase 发送不含内容的事件，例如：打开了哪个页面、录音时长区间、转写是否成功、选择了哪个订阅方案。同时会包含设备型号、系统版本、语言和一个随机的应用实例标识。绝不包含录音、文字、标题、标签或搜索内容。这些数据仅用于改进应用，不与你的身份关联，也不用于广告。拒绝或关闭后不会发送任何统计。

购买

订阅和购买由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。我们看不到你的付款信息，只会从 Apple 得知你的方案是否有效。

权限

  • 麦克风：仅在你开始录音后使用。
  • 语音识别：用于在本机把录音转成文字。

你的权利

你可以随时删除单条录音，或删除应用以清除全部数据。关于匿名统计，你可以在设置中撤回同意。如需行使个人信息相关权利或有任何问题，请通过下方方式联系我们。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

变更与联系

政策如有变更，会在本页更新并注明日期。联系方式：github.com/somefork2/earmark/issues

Privacy Policy

Earmark (声签) records on Apple Watch and iPhone and turns recordings into text. Our rule is simple: your recordings and text belong to you.

What we don't collect

  • No account. We run no servers that store your data.
  • Recordings, transcripts, titles, tags, marks and searches stay on your Apple Watch and iPhone and are never sent to us.
  • Speech-to-text runs on your iPhone with Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is not uploaded anywhere.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA), no cross-app tracking, no data sales.

Watch to iPhone transfer

Recordings travel directly between your own watch and iPhone through Apple's WatchConnectivity. The watch deletes its copy only after the iPhone confirms it has saved it.

iCloud backup (optional, off by default)

If you turn on iCloud backup in Settings, recordings and text are stored in your own iCloud account and handled by Apple under its privacy policy. We cannot access them.

Anonymous usage statistics (only with your consent)

On first launch we ask whether you want to share anonymous usage statistics; you can turn this off at any time in Settings. If you agree, the app sends content-free events through Google Analytics for Firebase, for example which screen was opened, recording length ranges, whether a transcription succeeded and which plan was chosen, together with device model, OS version, language and a random app-instance identifier. Audio, text, titles, tags and searches are never included. This data is used only to improve the app, is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising. If you decline or turn it off, nothing is sent.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details; Apple only tells the app whether your plan is active.

Permissions

  • Microphone: used only after you start a recording.
  • Speech recognition: used to turn recordings into text on your iPhone.

Your choices

You can delete any recording at any time, or delete the app to remove all data. You can withdraw consent to statistics in Settings. For any privacy request or question, contact us below.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page is updated with a new date. Contact: github.com/somefork2/earmark/issues
